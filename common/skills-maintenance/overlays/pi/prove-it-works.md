# `prove-it-works` semantic overlay

## Preserve

- Keep automatic invocation before declaring implementation or configuration work complete.
- Verify the user's real claim against the real artifact or running path, not a build, cache, timestamp, tool summary, or agent self-report.
- Choose the cheapest direct observation that covers the full claim, inspect delegated artifacts directly, and report verification blockers honestly.
- Respect task permissions and avoid creating permanent verification artifacts unless the work benefits from them.
- Preserve the bundled license and Pi metadata.

## Replace or omit from upstream

- Remove Cursor-only invocation and composition assumptions.
- Do not require a new script for every check; deterministic scripts are preferred when their reuse justifies keeping them.

## Acceptance

Completion claims name the direct observation performed and do not overstate builds or indirect proxies as behavioral proof.
